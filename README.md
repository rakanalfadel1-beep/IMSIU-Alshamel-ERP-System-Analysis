# 🏢 Enterprise Information Systems Analysis: Alshamel ERP (IMSIU)

## 📌 Project Overview
This foundational Information Systems project presents a comprehensive field study and systems analysis of the **Alshamel ERP System** deployed at the **Deanship of Information Technology and E-Learning** at Imam Mohammad Ibn Saud Islamic University (IMSIU). Developed by Adaptive TechSoft (ATS), the system transitions administrative operations from legacy paper-based workflows into an integrated, real-time centralized data warehouse spanning Financial Management, Human Resources, and Warehouse Management.

## 🔍 Key System Insights & Scope Analyzed
* **Centralized Data Warehouse:** Unified data repository connecting HR, Finance, and Inventory records in real time to eliminate data silos and paper archiving risks.
* **Automated Payroll & Government Integration:** Linked biometric attendance and leave records with Ministry of Human Resources regulations, automating salary calculations and direct disbursement via the Ministry of Finance's **Sarf** system.
* **Cross-System Interoperability:** Analyzed integrations between Alshamel ERP, the **Banner** academic system (linking faculty IDs to courses), the **Wafi** employee self-service portal, and **Jira** for IT incident ticketing.
* **Risk, Constraints & Vendor Analysis:** Evaluated commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) ERP trade-offs, including licensing and maintenance dependencies, customization constraints, data residency considerations, and operational continuity risks.

## 👨‍💻 My Contributions & Field Research
As part of the research team, I contributed to primary requirements gathering and systems documentation through on-site engagement with the Deanship's Business Solutions team:
* **Field Interviews & Discovery:** Conducted on-site interviews with IT Deanship specialists to map real-world administrative workflows, stakeholder hierarchies, and system dependencies.
* **Scope & Process Documentation:** Co-authored the formal scope statement, stakeholder analysis, and organizational architecture mapping (modeled via SimpleMind).
* **Technical & Risk Evaluation:** Documented system integration points, maintenance workflows, and operational constraints inherent to large-scale university ERP infrastructures.
